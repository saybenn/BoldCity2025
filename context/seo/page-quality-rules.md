# Page quality and publishing rules

Homepage intent: Water Damage Cleanup in Jacksonville. Core water page: the complete extraction/drying/restoration process. Mold: moisture source, containment, removal and HEPA cleanup. Air duct cleaning: one substantive core route, without automatic per-city expansion. Other existing service and area URLs are retained.

New pages need an advertised service, a distinct customer question and enough supported information to answer it. Core 30 is a research framework, not a publication quota. Reuse accurate shared process descriptions; never duplicate a whole page with a substituted city name. Do not add testing, chemical treatment, guarantees, precise response times or credentials to match a query.

Local content follows `local-content-standard.md` and `local-research.md`. Authentic project imagery can demonstrate visible work without a fabricated location, customer history or completed outcome. Review excerpts must come from a directly verified Google profile or authorized export and retain exact source attribution. A logo or avatar cannot verify review text or an aggregate rating.

Titles/descriptions/H1s must identify the page's actual purpose. Internal links should lead to useful service details, established area hubs and contact. Check canonical, sitemap, metadata and lead tracking before a route is considered ready.
