# Local content standard

Seven established area hubs and fourteen existing water/mold children are retained. `AREAS` owns identities and supported coverage. `LOCAL_DETAILS` owns area-specific source links, planning guidance and service questions. `PAGE_OVERRIDES` combines those explicit details with shared, accurate service process data. No local case studies are published because project records were not supplied.

Each area/service page has distinct intent metadata, a service H1, a source-backed regional paragraph, practical visit planning, a specific service introduction, relevant FAQs, related service links, sibling/hub links and tracked contact options. A source's regional flood statement is not evidence that Bold City completed a job there.

Add an area only after confirming coverage and collecting a real local need and primary source. Add a service/area combination only when its content can help customers beyond repeating the city name. All photos use descriptions of visible subjects; no city is appended to an alt text without location evidence. Current flood warnings, zoning, insurance and legal requirements need renewed research before use.
